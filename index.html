01 · My first app

Pet Breeder Genealogy Tracker

Progressive web app · Firebase

A hamster business grew to almost 200 animals, and related hamsters must not be bred for at least three generations. This app records every hamster, traces their bloodlines and answers one question instantly: can these two breed?

Problem

About 200 hamsters, and a three-generation breeding rule that is impossible to track by memory.

Solution

A mobile-first web app that stores every hamster, links parents and checks any pair automatically.

Result

Works on iPhones with no App Store release, with real-time sync so everyone sees the same data.

03 · Group project

Malaysia Photovoltaic Energy Predictor

Machine learning · GIS · TypeScript

A map-based web app that predicts solar energy yield across Malaysia. A model trained by our team runs inside the browser, so predictions need no backend.

  • In-browser XGBoost inference over 8,760 hours of weather data to predict solar yield.
  • Custom HTML5 Canvas renderer with viewport culling, so large power grids and AHP-scored heatmaps stay smooth.
  • Trained on Google Colab. When free-tier sessions kept dying, we checkpointed progress to Drive and resumed.
  • Google Maps integration to explore locations on the map.

04 · Final year project

Accessible Navigation with CV and AR

Computer vision · AR · iOS

A pedestrian app for wheelchair, walker and cane users that cares what the pavement is like, not just which way to turn. It plans around barriers, spots hazards through the camera, and draws the route onto the ground in AR.

Problem

Walkways in Malaysia are rarely friendly to mobility aids, and no single app covers routing, hazards and guidance.

Solution

Barrier-aware routing, on-device hazard detection and AR guidance, together in one iOS app.

Result

Built and tested on a real iPhone through a CI pipeline, even though my MacBook can't run the latest Xcode.
